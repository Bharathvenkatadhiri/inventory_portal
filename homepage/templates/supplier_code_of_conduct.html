Every manufacturer on ManufactureHub is expected to meet the standards below. Buyers rely on this code to source with confidence, and we verify against it as part of onboarding.

Quality and capability

Represent your processes, materials, tolerances and capacity accurately. Only quote on requirements you can deliver to the stated specification and timeline.

Fair dealing

Quote transparently, honor agreed prices and lead times, and communicate promptly if a delivery date is at risk. Do not use a buyer's drawings or specifications outside the scope of the order.

Confidentiality

Files shared through the platform are confidential. Access them only to prepare a quote or fulfil an awarded order, and respect any NDA terms attached to a requirement.

Compliance

Operate in accordance with applicable labor, safety and environmental regulations. Keep your business, GST and certification details current in your ManufactureHub profile.

Questions

If you have questions about this code, please contact us.
