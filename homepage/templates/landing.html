The network

One document, two views

When your supplier is on MakeSetu too, the purchase order you raise arrives in their Sales module as an incoming order. It's the same record, so nobody retypes it and both sides see the same status.

Buyer and supplier aren't account types. They describe a relationship between two companies, and each company keeps its own terms. If you also buy from your customer, that's a second relationship with its own terms.
