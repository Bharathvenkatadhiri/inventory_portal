{% comment %} Tells the web app's other open tabs who this page was rendered for. All tabs share one session cookie, so a page rendered signed out (after logging out, or the session expiring) means every tab is signed out, and a page rendered for someone else means another person signed in. The app's tabs listen on the same channel (watchSessionInOtherTabs in frontend/packages/api) and go to the sign-in page or reload at once, instead of on their next request. Carries only the user's numeric id, never anything else. {% endcomment %}
