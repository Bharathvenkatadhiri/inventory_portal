Strength:

Only strong passwords are accepted. Your password needs:

  • ○At least 8 characters
  • ○An uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • ○A lowercase letter (a-z)
  • ○A number (0-9)
  • ○A special character (! @ # $ % & …)
  • ○No easy patterns (aaa, 1111, abcd, 4321)
  • ·Not a common password (even with numbers or symbols added), and not too close to your name or email (checked when you submit)
  • ·Different from your last 5 passwords (checked when you submit)
