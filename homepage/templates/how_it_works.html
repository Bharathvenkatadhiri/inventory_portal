Getting started

Five steps to your first order

  1. 01

    Create your login

    Your name, work email and a password. We email you a code to confirm the address.

  2. 02

    Register your company

    Enter your GSTIN. We check it and fill in your legal name, address and PAN. You become the company's Org Admin.

  3. 03

    Choose what you use

    Buying (purchase orders, GRN, bills), selling (quotations, sales orders, invoices, dispatch), or both, and a plan.

  4. 04

    Invite your team

    Office staff get full roles: Purchase, Sales, Accounts and so on. Shop-floor staff get light roles, like Machine Operator or Store Keeper, that cost much less. Group people into teams such as Purchase, Store or QC.

  5. 05

    Connect your partners

    Invite a supplier or customer, or find a company already on MakeSetu. Once they accept, documents flow between you.

Customers and suppliers

Relationships, not account types

A manufacturer buys raw material from its suppliers and sells finished parts to its customers. Two companies can even buy from each other. So on MakeSetu "buyer" and "supplier" only describe a relationship between two companies.

Each relationship has its own payment terms, credit limit and codes, and it starts once the other side accepts, or once your vendor approval passes. A purchase order you raise to a connected supplier is the same record they see as an incoming sales order.

Every day

How work moves through the system

Buying

  1. Purchase raises a PO to an approved supplier
  2. Store posts the goods receipt (GRN)
  3. QC inspects the material
  4. Accounts matches the supplier's bill and pays it

Making

  1. The planner sets up the bill of materials
  2. A work order issues material from stock
  3. The shop floor records what was made
  4. QC passes the finished parts into stock

Selling

  1. Sales sends a quotation
  2. The accepted quotation becomes a sales order
  3. Store dispatches against the order
  4. Accounts raises the GST invoice and records the payment

Who can do what

Every action is checked three ways

For example, before someone can raise a purchase order to a supplier:

  1. Their role in the company they're working in allows creating purchase orders.
  2. Their company has buying switched on, and its plan includes the module.
  3. The supplier has an active relationship with their company.
