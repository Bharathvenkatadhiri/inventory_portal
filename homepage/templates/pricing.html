Pricing questions

Do I pay separately to buy and to sell?

No. Your company registers once and switches on buying, selling or both on any plan. Buyer and supplier are relationships with other companies, not separate accounts.

What's the difference between full and light users?

Full users are office staff: purchase, sales, accounts, the owner. Light users do one or two simple tasks on the shop floor: a store keeper recording goods received, a machine operator entering what was made, a QC inspector marking pass or fail, security at the gate, a salesperson booking orders on the phone. Light users can't approve anything or see finance reports. Each plan includes some free; extra ones are ₹{{ plans.0.extra_light_user_monthly }} a month each.

How do users and teams work?

Users are one pool for your whole company. Create teams such as Purchase, Sales, Store, Production and Accounts, and put anyone in as many teams as you like: each person counts once. Teams route approvals to the team head, send alerts to the right people and group reports by department. Need more teams? Growth has 5 and Pro has no limit.

Why give shop-floor staff their own login?

So nobody shares a login like "store@company". You see who entered what, and stock stays accurate because work is recorded when it happens. People from your customers and suppliers never count as your users: they use their own company's account.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up when you need work orders, QC, job work or MRP, and everything you've already entered stays where it is.

Is there a fee per order or invoice?

No. The plan covers everything listed above, however many documents you raise.
