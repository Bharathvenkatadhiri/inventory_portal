About ManufactureHub

Built for the people who make things.

ManufactureHub is where engineering buyers and manufacturers meet — one place to turn a drawing into a quote, and a quote into a finished part, without the back-and-forth of calls and spreadsheets.

Our Mission

Make custom manufacturing as easy to buy as it is to build.

Sourcing a custom part shouldn't take three weeks of emails and follow-up calls. We built ManufactureHub to connect people who need parts made — for metal cutting, bending, CNC machining, and more — with the manufacturers who can make them, so a requirement can become a quote, and a quote can become a delivery, all in one place.
