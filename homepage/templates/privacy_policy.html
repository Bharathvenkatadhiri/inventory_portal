This Privacy Policy explains how [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("MakeSetu", "we", "us"), registered office at [REGISTERED ADDRESS], collects, uses, shares and protects personal information in connection with the MakeSetu platform (the "Platform"). It applies to Buyers, Suppliers, team members invited to a company account, and anyone else who uses the Platform. By using the Platform, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account and company information: name, email address, phone number, and — for company accounts — company name, address, GSTIN and related GST verification details (legal name, registration status, state and PIN code), business type, and team member roles (for accounts with multiple invited users).

RFQ, quote and order content: part drawings and CAD files (e.g. STEP, IGES, STL, DXF, DWG), specifications, quotes, purchase orders, GST tax invoices, production updates, quality/certification documents, and messages (including file attachments) exchanged with counterparties through the Platform.

Supplier profile information: manufacturing capabilities, certifications, machine list, facility photos, and other information a Supplier chooses to publish on their profile.

Usage and security information: IP address, browser information, login timestamps, and a browser-session signal we use to detect when a session cookie is being used from an unexpected browser, so we can log that session out as a security precaution.

2. Cookies

We use only the cookies strictly necessary to operate the Platform: a session cookie (to keep you signed in) and a CSRF-protection cookie (to protect forms from cross-site request forgery). We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies. Your session cookie expires when you close your browser or after a period of inactivity.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate core features — matching RFQs to Suppliers, enabling quotes, generating purchase orders and GST tax invoices, and tracking order production status.
  • Verify the identity and GST registration status of company accounts.
  • Send transactional communications, such as email verification codes, RFQ and quote notifications, order and payment-status updates, and billing notices for your subscription plan.
  • Maintain the security of the Platform, including detecting suspicious session activity and keeping an internal audit log of account-affecting actions taken by our staff (e.g. account verification or deactivation).
  • Comply with legal obligations, including tax and accounting record-keeping.

We do not use your data to serve advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Who We Share Information With

We share information only as follows:

  • With your trading counterparty: when you post an RFQ, submit a quote, or transact on an order, relevant details (including drawings and contact information needed to fulfil the order) are shared with the specific Buyer(s) or Supplier(s) involved in that RFQ or order — never with the wider platform.
  • Infrastructure providers: we use Amazon Web Services for file storage (uploaded drawings, certificates, order documents and message attachments) and for sending transactional email, under contracts that restrict their use of your data to providing these services to us.
  • GST verification providers: if you provide a GSTIN, it (and associated company legal-name/address details) may be sent to a GST verification service to confirm registration status.
  • Payment processor: subscription-plan payments are processed by our payment processor. We do not share order-level payment details with any processor, because order payments between Buyers and Suppliers are settled directly between those parties and are not processed by MakeSetu — see our Terms of Service.
  • Legal and compliance: where required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a governmental request.

5. Data Storage, Security and Transfers

Files you upload are stored using encrypted, access-controlled cloud storage and are served over encrypted connections. Message attachments are only accessible to participants in that conversation. We restrict staff access to personal data and log account-affecting staff actions for accountability. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Depending on the cloud region configured for our infrastructure providers, data may be stored or processed outside the country in which you are located; where that occurs, we take steps to ensure it is handled consistently with this Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain account, RFQ, quote, order and invoicing records for as long as your account is active and thereafter for as long as needed to meet accounting, tax and legal record-keeping obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security audit trails. If you close your account or request deletion, we delete or anonymise personal data that is no longer required for these purposes within a reasonable time.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable data protection law (including, for users in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access and correct your personal information — most profile fields can be updated directly from your account settings.
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request erasure of your personal data, subject to our legitimate need to retain certain records (e.g. completed orders, invoices, audit logs) for legal, tax and security purposes.
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint about how your data is handled.

We do not currently offer fully automated self-service deletion or data-export; to exercise any of these rights, contact us as described below and we will action your request manually within a reasonable time.

8. Children's Privacy

The Platform is a business-to-business service intended for use by individuals aged 18 and over acting on behalf of a company. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
