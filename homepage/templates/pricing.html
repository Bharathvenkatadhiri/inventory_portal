Pricing questions

Who's on a team, and who can do what?

The person who registers the company is its Owner, who alone handles the subscription and billing. Buyer companies add Admins, Procurement and Viewers; manufacturer companies add Admins, Sales, Operations and Viewers. Every active person counts toward the plan's users, Viewers included.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Moving to a smaller plan applies straight away. An upgrade, or a switch to yearly billing, starts as soon as we've confirmed your payment.

What happens when I reach a limit?

Nothing you've already done is affected. You can't post another RFQ, send another quote, add another person or upload past your storage until next month (for monthly limits) or an upgrade.

Are there any transaction fees?

No transaction fees on awarded orders — your plan covers everything listed above.
