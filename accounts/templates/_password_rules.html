{% comment %} Live password checks for a "password + confirm password" pair. Include it once in the form, after both fields: {% include "_password_rules.html" with password_id=form.password1.id_for_label confirm_id=form.password2.id_for_label submit_id="register-submit-btn" %} It renders the criteria under the password field and the match message under the confirm field (both moved next to their fields by the script), and keeps the submit button disabled until every rule passes and the two match. The server applies the same rules and more (settings.AUTH_PASSWORD_VALIDATORS); this is only so people see them as they type. {% endcomment %}

  • ○An uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • ○A lowercase letter (a-z)
  • ○A number (0-9)
  • ○A special character (! @ # $ % & …)
  • ·Not a common password (even with numbers or symbols added), and not too close to your name or email
