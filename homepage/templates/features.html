Buying · every plan

Purchase

Raise purchase orders to approved suppliers, post goods receipts (GRN) as material arrives, and enter the supplier's bill against what was ordered and received.

  • Purchase orders with GST worked out from the place of supply
  • Goods receipts against each order line
  • Purchase bills matched to the order and the receipt

Selling · every plan

Sales

Quote your customers, turn accepted quotations into sales orders, and dispatch against them. Orders from customers on MakeSetu arrive already filled in.

  • Quotations and sales orders
  • Dispatch against each order line
  • Incoming orders from connected customers

Store · every plan

Inventory and documents

Stock by item and warehouse, updated by every receipt, issue and dispatch. Delivery challans, returns, and debit and credit notes are included. Starter covers 1 warehouse, Growth 3 and Pro 10. Growth adds batch and serial tracking with barcodes.

Accounts · every plan

GST invoicing, e-way bill and Tally

GST invoices from dispatches, e-way bills, payments and ledgers, GSTR reports and Tally export. E-invoice (IRN) is included from Growth and available as an add-on on Starter.

Production

BOM, work orders and costing

Every plan has single-level bills of materials and production entry. Growth adds multi-level BOMs, work orders, production costing and scrap.

Quality and job work · Growth

QC, inspection and job work

Inspect incoming material and finished parts, and hold back what fails. Send material out for job work on a challan, track it back, and file ITC-04.

Approvals · Growth and Pro

Approvals

Growth requires one approval before orders, bills or payments go out. Pro adds multi-level approvals, so larger amounts can need more than one person.

Planning · Pro

MRP, scheduling and maintenance

Plan material and production from open orders, schedule the work, keep machines maintained, and see a full audit log of who changed what.

People

Roles for every desk

A role is a set of permissions, such as "create a purchase order" or "approve a sales order". Every company starts with Org Admin, Purchase, Sales, Store, Production Planner, Accounts and QC roles, and can change them. One person can work in more than one company, with a different role in each.

Light users are for the shop floor: Store Keeper, Machine Operator, QC Inspector, Security / Gate and Field Sales. They record one or two things, such as goods received, production done or a pass or fail, cost much less than full users, and can never approve or see finance.

Teams group people into departments such as Purchase, Store or QC, each with a head. A Purchase team's POs go to its head for approval, QC alerts go to the QC team, and reports show activity by department. Anyone can be in several teams and still counts once.
